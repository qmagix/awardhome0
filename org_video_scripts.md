# Organizer Video Scripts

Two videos, two jobs. The **marketing short** earns a busy director's attention
(embedded in the invitation email, social, trade booths — assume ~45 seconds of
patience). The **instructional video** earns follow-through after they say yes
(linked from the dashboard and the welcome email). Don't mix them: a pitch that
teaches is too long, a tutorial that sells is annoying.

Companion to `org_invite_draft.md` — the letter carries the personalization
("we loved your Tampa results"); the videos stay generic and reusable, except
for the optional per-org branded capture (see Production Notes).

---

## Video 1 — Marketing short (~60 seconds)

**Audience:** competition directors/owners, cold or warm.
**Goal:** one action — click the claim link in the invitation email.
**Key points, in order:** (1) your dancers already share their awards — put
your brand on every card they share; (2) zero work, zero cost, any file format; (3) permanent
searchable results end the "where are the 2023 results?" emails; (4) CTA:
claim link in the email.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Phone screen: a dancer's trophy-case page scrolling; a gold award card gets shared to Instagram. | "Right now, dancers from your competition are sharing their awards online. The question is — is your brand on them?" |
| 0:08–0:22 | AwardHome homepage leaderboards; zoom into award cards carrying org logos. | "AwardHome is the digital trophy case for competitive dance — over 900,000 awards from 2,700 events, across 14 competitions. Every award your event gives out becomes a permanent, shareable trophy card. With your brand on it." |
| 0:22–0:38 | Split screen: a director wading through "where can I find the 2023 results?" emails vs. a clean AwardHome event page. | "No more digging up old results for parents. Your events live forever, searchable, beautiful — right beside the biggest names in the industry." |
| 0:38–0:50 | A results file dragged onto the upload form; checkmark; trophy cards populate. | "And it costs you nothing — not money, not time. Send us your results in whatever format you have. CSV, PDF, even a database export. We handle everything." |
| 0:50–0:60 | Invitation email on screen; cursor clicks the claim button; account appears. Logo + awardhome.com. | "Your invitation email has your private access link — claiming your account takes about two minutes. Or just reply with a results file, and we'll build your page first." |

**Deliberately cut:** attendance analytics (strong in the letter, too abstract
for 60s) and the registration-button feature (tutorial material). Three points
land; six don't.

## Video 2 — Instructional walkthrough (~3½ minutes)

**Audience:** a director (or their assistant) who clicked the claim link.
**Goal:** first successful results upload + branding set up.
**Format:** screen recording with cursor, calm pace, chapter markers.

### 1. Getting in (0:00–0:25)
*Screen: invitation email → claim link → account page → dashboard.*

> "Welcome to AwardHome. Your invitation email contains your private access
> link — click it, choose a password, and your organizer account is live.
> That link is unique to your organization, so there's nothing to apply for
> and nothing to verify. You land on your dashboard."

### 2. Dashboard tour (0:25–0:55)
*Screen: Overview — stats cards, then Live Mockups; click through to a dancer
trophy case showing the org's awards.*

> "Up top: every award we already host from your events, and how many studios
> attended. The Live Mockups show your awards exactly as dancers and studios
> see them on their own pages — this is what your brand rides on."

### 3. Uploading results — the core (0:55–2:00)
*Screen: Update Event Results tab. Fill event name, date, location. Open the
format guidance box; download the CSV template; show its columns in a
spreadsheet; drag the file in; submit; the upload appears under Recent
Uploads as "AI Processing Soon".*

> "When an event wraps, send us the results here. Three quick fields — event
> name, dates, city — and the file. Any format works: CSV, Excel, PDFs,
> even a raw export from your scoring system's database, up to fifty
> megabytes. If you can export a spreadsheet, the template here shows the
> ideal shape: one row per routine per award — event, date, routine,
> placement, category, age division, dancers, studio. Full names rather than
> initials, and consistent studio spellings, mean your results go live
> faster — but send what you have, and our team handles the rest. You'll see
> the file listed here with its status; we parse it, verify it, and your
> results appear on dancer and studio pages, usually within days."

### 4. Branding (2:00–2:45)
*Screen: Branding & Icons — upload logo, drag size and transparency sliders,
watch the trophy-card preview update; then map a custom icon: crown for
Title, diamond for Platinum.*

> "Now the fun part. Upload your logo and set its size and visibility with
> these sliders — our design team then hand-fits it into the card's logo
> coin before it goes live, usually within a day. Below, you can map
> custom icons to your own award levels. Want a crown for Title winners and a
> diamond for Platinum? Every award becomes a little ad for your competition,
> long after the event ends."

### 5. Marketing profile (2:45–3:05)
*Screen: Marketing Profile tab — slogan, description, registration URL; then
a public results page showing the "Register for Next Year" button.*

> "Add a slogan, a description, and your registration link. That link becomes
> a register button right on your results pages — catching dancers at the
> exact moment they're proudest."

### 6. Wrap (3:05–3:25)
*Screen: Published Events list; then hello@awardhome.com on screen.*

> "That's the whole loop: results in, branded trophies out, forever. Your
> published events all live here. Questions, weird file formats, anything —
> hello@awardhome.com reaches a human. Welcome aboard."

## Production notes

- **Per-org branded capture (recommended for major targets):** stage the
  org's public logo in the branding dashboard **on the local instance, never
  prod** (a live logo would publicly imply a partnership that doesn't exist
  yet — set logo → record → delete, all locally). Capture ~15s of their real
  org page and a dancer trophy case wearing their logo; splice into the
  marketing short at 0:08–0:22 or attach as a GIF in the email. This is the
  video form of the letter's "we'll build you a live demo page" promise.
- The claim links in invitation emails are single-use and expire in 30 days —
  if a video drives a click after expiry, the error page tells them to reply
  to the email for a fresh link.
- Keep the marketing short generic (no org names) so one edit serves every
  send; personalization lives in the letter and the optional capture.
- Record UI at 1400px+ width (admin tables are tuned for it) and hide the
  browser bookmarks bar.
